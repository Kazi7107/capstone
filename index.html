CSS 496 Capstone · Kazim Syed

What will this car really cost you?

Redline is a web app for used car buyers. You enter a specific car, and it shows you the true 5-year cost of owning it: fuel, insurance, maintenance and repairs, registration and taxes, and depreciation, not just the sticker price.

This portfolio walks through the project from three angles: what it does for you as a buyer, how it was built, and what building it taught me.

Redline

5-year cost

What will this car really cost you?

Redline is a web app for used car buyers in Washington. Pick a specific car and it shows you the true cost of owning it for five years, not just the sticker price.

Depreciation, fuel, insurance, maintenance, repairs, and taxes and registration, all in one clear number you can compare.

Kazim SyedCSS 496 CapstoneUniversity of WashingtonAutumn 2026

Six costs.
One number.

The price on the windshield is only the start. Redline adds up everything you’ll pay over five years of ownership, so you can compare cars on what they actually cost you.

  1. 00 · not the whole story Sticker price

  3. 02 Fuel

  4. 03 Insurance

  5. 04 Maintenance & repairs

  6. 05 Taxes & registration

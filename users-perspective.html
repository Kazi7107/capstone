User's Perspective

What Redline does for you when you're shopping for a used car in Washington.

On this page

  1. What Redline Is
  2. The Problem It Solves
  3. What You Get
  4. Using Redline

What Redline Is

A short, plain-language intro to Redline: you pick a specific used car and see what it will really cost you to own it for five years.

The Problem It Solves

Why the sticker price misleads you, and how depreciation, insurance and repairs can make a “cheaper” car cost you more.

What You Get

The features you get: a 5-year total, a breakdown across six cost categories, and side-by-side comparisons of the cars you’re considering.

Using Redline

A walkthrough of how you use Redline, step by step, with annotated screenshots.
